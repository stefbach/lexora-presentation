✧ The Lexora philosophy

"You scan. The magic happens."

Lexora's founding idea fits in one sentence: the user no longer keys in the accounting — they trigger it. A photo of a receipt, an invoice PDF, a bank statement… and the accounting builds itself, then is driven through conversation.

Two entry points, a single intelligence

Whether you're at your desk or in the field, the same engine processes your document. You simply choose the channel.

▤ Route 1 — the web app

Drag & drop into Lexora

You drop an invoice, receipt or statement (PDF, JPEG, PNG, XLSX — 20 MB max) into Documents & OCR. The system analyzes and automatically files it in the right folder, by company and by type.

✈ Route 2 — Telegram, on the move

A photo to the Lexora bot

From your phone, you snap a photo of the receipt or statement and send it to the bot. Claude OCR extracts it and injects it directly into the accounting of the active company. Ideal for expense claims.

Vision extraction (Claude OCR)

The agent reads the document like a human: it identifies the supplier/client, the number, the date, the net/gross amount, the currency and the VAT. A control agent (Lex OCR) then compares the extraction against what was actually recorded and flags any inconsistency or duplicate.

Automatic posting to the Mauritian Chart of Accounts

A deterministic layer picks the right account (401/411 parties, 627 expenses, 512 bank…), detects the applicable TDS withholding and computes the net payable. For a GBC company, auto-tagging applies IAS 21 (currency), the 80% PER and spots related parties.

The entry builds and reconciles itself

The balanced accounting entry is generated, then the Lex Banque agent reconciles it with the matching bank transaction (8 cascading matching strategies). You only approve what deserves a human eye.

Compliance updates in real time

VAT, MRA deadlines, IFRS 9 ECL, GBC exposure, balance sheet: everything recalculates automatically. The dashboards and alerts instantly reflect the new document.

Then: "it's driven by conversation"

Once the accounting is built, you no longer click through menus — you talk to your business. In natural language, across three interchangeable channels, with a shared memory.

The 8 matching strategies (cascading)

  • Invoice number detected in the label → top priority
  • Multi-subset: N invoices → 1 transaction (2% tolerance)
  • Successive part-payments: N payments → 1 invoice
  • Fuzzy party matching (Jaccard ≥ 0.35, aliases, substring)
  • Accepted date offset: −60d to +180d

Deterministic classifications R01–R06

  • Internal transfers → account 580
  • Bank charges → 627 (non-matchable, R7)
  • MRA social charges → 444
  • Bulk/individual salaries → 421 / 4210
  • Shareholder current account repayment → 455
The AI is never blind: every suggestion shows a confidence score and the strategy used, then goes through human approval before the final matching.

⌘ MCP · Claude Desktop connector

Lexora inside Claude, on your computer

Beyond the app and Telegram, Lexora exposes itself as an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server. In practice: you connect your Lexora accounting to Claude Desktop, and you drive everything from a Claude conversation — on your Mac or PC.

Why it's powerful: Claude Desktop becomes a universal interface. It can read your companies, your invoices, your MRA deadlines and trigger Lexora actions at the same time as it works with your other tools (files, emails, other MCP connectors). Lexora becomes a building block of your augmented workstation.

The setup — "generate a key, run a command, done"

lexora.finance/client/direction/mcp-setup

Generate your personal key, run a command, done.

Generate a personal API key

Each key is tied to you, revocable at any time and hashed in the database — no one can see it in clear text, not even Lexora admins. Active keys: "MCP LEXORA BIS", "MCP MA CLE LEXORA".

Prerequisites on your computer

Claude Desktop installed + Node.js v18+ (brew install node).

A single command

Mac / Linux, in the Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://www.lexora.finance/install-mcp.sh | bash

Windows: equivalent PowerShell command. The script asks for the Lexora URL and the key.

Test

Quit and restart Claude Desktop, and type: "List my Lexora companies". It's connected.
